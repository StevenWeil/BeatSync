BeatSync

privacy policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what BeatSync does and does not do with your data.

What BeatSync Does

BeatSync listens to the sounds around you in real time to detect beats in music, then pulses your iPhone flashlight in sync. That is the entirety of what the app does.

Microphone

BeatSync uses your iPhone's microphone solely to detect beats in real time. Audio is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. The moment you stop the app, audio processing stops completely. We have no access to anything your microphone picks up.

Camera

BeatSync uses the rear camera flash as a flashlight. No photos or video are ever captured, recorded, or accessed. The camera itself is never opened — only the flash hardware is used.

Data Collection

BeatSync collects no personal data of any kind. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app is ever sent off your device.

Data Sharing

Because we collect no data, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to any third party — ever.

Children

BeatSync does not collect any information from anyone, including children. The app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
